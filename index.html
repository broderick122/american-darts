About

Played at the post

Roslyn American Darts is the American darts group that plays at Roslyn VFW Post 3498 in Abington, Pennsylvania. The hall is at 1140 Easton Road.

This page is a starting mock. The standings and the score sheet already work. The story of the group still needs to be written by the people who throw here.
