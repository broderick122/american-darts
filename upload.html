This is an experiment. A phone photo of handwriting is a hard thing for the browser to read. Digits get missed, names come out wrong, and a blank box can look like a mark. Nothing here is trusted until you look at it.

The reliable path is still the typed score sheet. Use that when the reading is a mess. The photo is not saved. Only the score you confirm is stored, in this browser for now.

At the end of the night, photograph the filled v2 sheet. Keep all four black corner squares in the frame, on a flat table, in even light. The page uses those marks to line the photo up with the printed boxes, then tries to read team names, the three game winners, and the inning digits.
